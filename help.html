Help centre

Frequently Asked Questions

Orders

How do I place an order?

Browse the collection, choose your size, add pieces to your shopping bag and proceed to checkout.

Can I save items for later?

Yes — tap the heart on any piece to add it to your wishlist. It stays saved on this device.

Do you offer promo codes?

Subscribers receive exclusive codes. Enter yours in the order summary on the shopping bag page.

Payment

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept M-Pesa, Airtel Money and credit or debit cards.

Can I shop in US dollars?

Yes. Switch currency in the footer settings; prices are shown at an indicative exchange rate and charged in KSh.

Shipping & returns

How long does shipping take?

Shipping takes 2 days within Kenya. Delivery is complimentary in Nairobi on orders over KSh 5,000.

Can I return an item?

Yes, you can return items within 7 days if they are in their original condition.

Client services

How do I contact customer support?

Email support@treqstore.com, call +254 724 398 102, or message us on WhatsApp from the contact page.

Can I book an in-store appointment?

Yes — use the store locator to find your nearest boutique.
